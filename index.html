How to run it

  1. W0

    Buy each firm's batch from the cards above. At 0 of 5 that is about 25 evals a firm. Once you hold 3 or more accounts, buy 3 to 5 at a time.

  2. W1–W2

    Evals resolve: two winning days pass one. Move passes into free slots. Extra passes wait for a slot, so overbuying only means a pass sits for a week.

  3. W2–W3

    Funded trade at 4:1, then 5:1 after one loss. Two losses and the account is gone; fill its slot from a waiting pass the same day.

  4. W4

    Payout 1 after the flip days: about $1,900 per Lucid account; Topstep takes $2,000 out (+$4k becomes +$2k).

  5. W6

    Payout 2: about $900 per Lucid account; Topstep takes $1,000 out (+$2k becomes +$1k). Five Lucid in profit pays about $9.5k, then $4.5k.

  6. W8

    Topstep payout 3 only after a good flip. After a bad one the account goes back to funded trading. Expect to lose some funded after the third cycle.

  7. Every 2 weeks

    Recount each firm: accounts held, waiting passes, accounts in profit. Re-buy from the table and check the yardsticks.

What the numbers assume

  • Timing. An eval resolves within a week, a funded account within a week of activation, and payouts land 2 and 4 weeks after it is in profit (6 for a third). Holiday and roll weeks run slower.
  • Funded to in profit. 1 − (1 − trade win)tries. Two tries because a $1,000 risk fits twice in a 50K account's $2,000 drawdown.
  • Payouts. 50% of profit per request, capped at $2,000 on a 50K, 90% to you (LucidFlex payouts, Topstep payout policy). The Lucid figures are the chat's own; Topstep's are $2,000, $1,000 and $500 withdrawals at 90%.
  • Prices. Topstep 50K is $49 a month plus a $149 activation on the Standard path (pricing). Lucid 50K runs about $70 with a code and $130–175 without. Use your checkout price.
  • Buying rule. Every second week the model buys enough evals to fill the open slots, counting slots that free within two weeks. It caps a batch at 25, or at 5 once a firm holds 3 accounts.
  • After the last payout. The account counts as lost and its slot frees up, as in the chat's "expect to lose some funded after the third cycle". Topstep accounts without a good flip are lost after payout 2. Any account that keeps trading is extra upside.
  • Fees. Put platform, data or subscription fees in Monthly fee per account. It is charged every 4 weeks for every held account and open eval.
  • Bad luck. The tiles are averages. The bad-luck line replays the 12 weeks 1,000 times, passing and failing whole accounts at random, and shows the worst run in 10.
